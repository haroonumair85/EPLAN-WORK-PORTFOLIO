Jizan Airport DMCP

Synchronization control system · Saudi Arabia

A control panel that synchronizes seven 13.8 kV generators and a bus tie across two busbars, sheds and restores load through the airport's MV switchgear, and reports everything to a central HMI. I built the full 90-sheet package and took it through five for-approval revisions.

  • Synchronizing and load-sharing circuits for 8 DEIF AGC 150 controllers, with CT, line-PT and bus-PT inputs
  • Load-shed logic I/O for two DEIF ALC-4 PLCs, wired to MV breaker status and command signals
  • Ethernet network to a 15" AGI 415 HMI and PC, plus Modbus RTU (RS-485) links
  • 66 interface relays, 70 fuse disconnectors and 688 terminals, all tagged and listed in the BOM

Addoha Poultry

LV synchronizing panel · Middle East

A 5550 A main switchboard that parallels three generators with the utility supply through a bus coupler. I designed the power and control schematics and the GA, and carried the set through 11 revisions to as-built, including a mid-project switch of ATS controller from Lovato ATL601 to DEIF AGC 150.

  • Generator and utility incomers on Eaton IZMX40 2500 A withdrawable ACBs with motor operator, shunt trip and UVR
  • 4 DEIF AGC 150 controllers handling synchronizing, load sharing, breaker commands and genset start/stop
  • Mechanical and electrical interlocking between incomers and bus coupler
  • Five-section line-up drawn to scale, with busbars sized for 100 kA (1 s)

Shura Island Hotels

HVAC MCC / EMCC · HC-02 and HC-03 · Saudi Arabia

Motor control centres and emergency MCCs for the HVAC plant of two resort hotels, alongside the MDBs, SMDBs, DBs, ATS and synchronizing panels on the same job. The schematics cover every starter type the plant needed, with BMS status and remote control on each feeder.

  • DOL, star-delta, VFD and dual-speed (low/high) motor starter circuits
  • Earth-leakage relays, overload and trip indication on each feeder
  • Manual/auto selection, with run, trip and selector status sent to the BMS

ATS Panel 1000 A

Automatic transfer switch · utility / generator

A mains-to-generator transfer panel for a 480 V system. I designed the power scheme, the transfer controller I/O and the breaker control, then generated the terminal and material lists from the schematic.
